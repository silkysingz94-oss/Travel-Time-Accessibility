Methodology and interpretation
Road data: Major and minor roads are retrieved from OpenStreetMap using the highway classification tags.

Travel-time model: Estimated distance is calculated as average speed multiplied by travel time.

Accessibility area: The displayed area is an approximate circular reach area around the origin. It is not a network-based isochrone and does not represent verified road travel time.

Limitations: This prototype does not yet model road direction restrictions, turn restrictions, real-time traffic, surface conditions, vehicle-specific access, or actual road speeds.
